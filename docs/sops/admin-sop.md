# StudySearch – Platform Admin SOP

**Version:** 1.56 (reviewed for platform release v2.15.385)  
**Audience:** Platform Admin (role rank 6)  
**Change control:** CF-247  
**Rule:** this SOP is reviewed with every release; a change to a screen, an automation or where a person decides changes it, raises its version and is recorded below. The in-app copy is Guides › Platform Admin SOP.

---

## 1. Purpose

How the Platform Admin runs StudySearch day to day: the approvals only you can give, the budgets and models you own, and the checks that keep automatic admission trustworthy.

## 2. What you own

- Admitting university adapters (Layer 2/3 › Universities)
- Approving central pages: fee schedules, English policies, academic calendars
- AI models, their order, AI admission and the AI allowances
- Firecrawl and OpenRouter budgets and service keys
- Onboarding settings, Retry and Stop
- Users and roles; new countries; go-live

## 3. How the platform automates, and where a person decides

## 4. Rules that never change

1. A value entered by hand is never overwritten by automation. Release it on the record to let automation update it again.
2. A passing test switches nothing on by itself. Approving a central page, activating an AI model or adding it to the order is a separate step a person takes, with a reason. Admitting an adapter's passing fields is automatic only while a Platform Admin has switched on Admit automatically (Layer 2/3 › Operations › Onboarding settings, with a reason); a field that did not pass is never admitted.
3. AI models are pinned and qualified for each task; nothing routes to an untested model. An AI answer counts only where its quote is on the stored page.
4. AI spending is watched, not capped, while OpenRouter limits are set to Observe: Models & services › OpenRouter shows the balance, today's spend for each AI use against its daily guard and the credit floor, and a notice appears when one is passed. Nothing stops until a Platform Admin switches the limits to stop.
5. Every background job is a scheduled job in the automation catalogue or a task in Task manager, with its controls. Something running that you cannot see there is a fault: report it.
6. Work oldest first, give a reason for every decision, and never decide from memory: open the stored page or document.

## 6. Weekly

- Models & services › AI & OpenRouter: OpenRouter balance (read every 15 minutes) and each AI use against its daily guard (change a guard there); Models & services › Toolsets & limits: Firecrawl balance against the month and the spend by day; the adapter AI allowance (proposals and US$ a day). Top up or adjust a guard before the balance reaches the credit floor. _(Models & services)_
- Models & services › AI & OpenRouter › Scholarship extraction: cost today, the model order, and any model waiting to be qualified or added. A model joins the order only after it qualifies, and only when you add it. _(Models & services › AI & OpenRouter)_
- 10-minute field check, 1 of 4. Coverage & completeness › Attributes › Stalled fields: each Not moving row has its cause and action. Pages not read yet: Fill now. Pages do not state it: give the listed universities a central page or adapter rule (Layer 2/3 › Universities › Builder), then Release holds and fill with a reason; or lower the target with a reason if they do not publish it. No reader yet: a build task (set the target to 0 until it exists). _(Coverage & completeness › Attributes)_
- 10-minute field check, 2 of 4. Every field by country: read across each row; a country far behind the others on a field gets the same treatment as a stalled field. _(Coverage & completeness › Attributes)_
- 10-minute field check, 3 of 4. Fill the gaps (standing queue): a run in the last 6 hours and values filled in 7 days. Cross-check differences: many on one field means a reading rule to fix, not courses to change one by one; a university's own delivery phrase goes in Delivery wording, with a reason. _(Coverage & completeness › Attributes)_
- 10-minute field check, 4 of 4. Platform health: no Coverage sweep "below target and not moving" warning older than a week; Go to opens Stalled fields. _(Platform health › Health checks)_
- Layer 2/3 › Enrichment plan: automatic plans open and the toolset shares left; the on-demand allowance used. For a university a customer or the website asks about, Open plan, read each step's estimate and Run now the steps worth their cost, with a reason. Switch automatic plans off (with a reason) if a share is needed elsewhere. Then open Plans that need a person: each line says what stopped (for example an adapter that read nothing); open the plan or the Adapter builder. On Enrich fields › Fee reader (AI), check the providers with problems and widen the scope (Settings › Pipeline settings › Enrichment) when the current one reads well. On Settings › Backup & restore, Compare the last daily backup: a provider published or a setting changed that nobody decided is put back with Put back chosen and a reason. _(Layer 2/3 Build & enrich › Enrichment plan)_
- Users & roles: lowest role that does the job; expiry dates for contractors. _(Users & roles)_
- Layer 2/3 › Operations: the stage counts, the pace and the estimate for the queue. Adjust "How many at once" (100 now) in Onboarding settings if credits run low or the queue is slow; queue universities not yet in onboarding when new ones appear. _(Layer 2/3 Build & enrich › Universities)_

## 8. What to pay attention to

- The credit and limit bar on every page.
- Universities stopped or needing a person on Layer 2/3 › Universities, and how long they have waited; the pace of the queue.
- OpenRouter balance against the credit floor, and any AI use past its daily guard.
- Approvals waiting: a growing list means universities get no values from their central pages.
- Held scholarships in the Inbox: a sudden rise after a rule or model change.
- The same automation failing three or more runs in a row.
- Scholarships withdrawn by the nightly review: a jump means a rule changed or a site moved its pages.

## 9. Escalation

- A job that fails the same way after a fix, a worker refusing work ("invalid key", "nonce"), or data written where it should not be: pause the job, note what you saw, raise it with the platform engineer with the change-control reference (CF-247).
