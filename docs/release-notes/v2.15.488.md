# v2.15.488 — Intake automation progress in Process results

Platform Admin conditional authorisation 6 Nov 2026 09:44 (PR #889).

## What changes

- Process results opens with an **Automation progress: intake dates** section: job state, last and next run, the funnel from selection to publication, one row per university, and history.
- Failures show their class, exact reason, next action and next revisit. Rechecks caused by a changed setting or source update are not counted as failures.
- The section reads the platform's existing progress observer. There is no second monitor.
- Pause and Resume use the existing job control.

## Not changed

- Intake selection, admission and publication rules.
- The Website and Zoho consumer API.
