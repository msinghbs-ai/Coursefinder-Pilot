# PIM Admin v2.15.377 — Scheduled checks inside their time limits

Date: 31 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- No screen or operator step changed.

## Fixes

- **Waste guard and AI match preparation:**
  - **The fault:** both jobs checked each university's settings once for every course, AI-match row or flag. As those tables grew, the jobs reached their 2-minute limit and failed. Waste guard failed 14 of 24 runs on 30 October.
  - **The fix:** each university is now checked once per run. The flags raised and the work prepared are the same, proven against the previous version on live data.
  - **Run times:** waste guard's refresh takes about 15 seconds, down from 115 to 120 seconds; AI match preparation about 19 seconds, down from a median of 48.
- **Consumer API:** unchanged.
