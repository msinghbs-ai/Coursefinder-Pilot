# PIM Admin v2.15.376 — Course outcome register

Date: 30 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Catalogue › Entry requirements › Resolution inspection** (Pipeline Operator and above): the **Course outcome register**.
  - Every active course of the entry-read countries has one outcome for academic entry requirements:
    - admitted course requirement;
    - admitted provider resolution;
    - awaiting Layer 4 review;
    - verified: no requirement on the page;
    - no official course URL;
    - identity unresolved or mismatched;
    - source unreadable;
    - excluded, with its reason.
  - Counts by country, and **Download CSV** with every course, its reason, its page and its Layer 4 item.
- **Rebuilt by the job AER course outcome register.** Its interval, download page size and stale mark are settings under Platform upkeep. The register shows when it was built.
- **Read only:** it admits, reads, queues and decides nothing.
- **Consumer API:** unchanged.

## Fixes

- None.
