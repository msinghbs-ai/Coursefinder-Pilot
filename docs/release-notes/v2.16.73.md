# PIM Admin v2.16.73 — Course links move to the current year by themselves

Date: 20 Nov 2026

## Changed

- Course link pages held in Layer 4 because their address carries an old year (such as `/2023/`) are now tried at 2027, then 2026, then 2025, then at the address held. The newest page that opens on the university's own site, is not a "not found" or "no longer offered" page, and shows the course's exact CRICOS code is admitted by itself, and its page is stored at once for the other readers. No Firecrawl and no AI are used. Pages that prove nothing stay in Layer 4 with what was tried.
- Layer 4 Review › By university: a rule group of Course link cards has Preview & Settle Batch (one decision, a reason, and `SETTLE n` typed to confirm; each card is decided on its own).
