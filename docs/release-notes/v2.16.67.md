# PIM Admin v2.16.67 — 4-stage pipeline on Platform Health, Dashboard cards by country, Layer 4 rule groups and ? help

Date: 20 Nov 2026

Platform Health now shows where data is in the pipeline and where it is stuck, the Dashboard gives each role its own view with completeness by country, Layer 4 groups the waiting cards by the rule that holds them, and every main screen has a short guide behind a ?.

## What's new

- Platform Health shows the 4-stage pipeline under the summary: Official Registers, Page Capture, AI Extraction & Auto-Clean and Live Catalogue. Each stage shows its status (green running normally, amber slowed or due, red stopped), what is queued and how many of its checks pass. Select a stage to read what it is doing and open the screen that controls it.
- The Dashboard shows five cards with icons: system status, completeness with a bar for Australia, New Zealand and Canada, decisions waiting on you, active blockers, and today's AI spend against the daily cap. Operators also see Active failures. A Viewer or Counsellor sees catalogue search only, and no pipeline figures are read for that role.
- Layer 4 Inbox: Waiting, grouped by rule, on the left groups the cards on the page by the check that holds them. Preview the batch lists a group's cards and Show only these narrows the list to them. Each card is still decided on its own action bar.
- A ? on the Dashboard, Platform Health, Layer 4 Review, Courses and Providers headers opens three steps for that screen and a link to the Platform guide.

## Fixed

- Platform Health and the top-bar health dot read stored figures, rebuilt every 5 minutes, instead of counting the work tables on every open. If the stored figures are more than 15 minutes old, the screen counts live, as before.
- Workers that find nothing to do no longer add a log line on every call: a run of idle calls keeps one line, with the number of calls on it, for up to 15 minutes (a setting).

## Good to know

- A stage's link opens the existing screen where its controls already are; nothing new runs from Platform Health.
