# PIM Admin v2.16.68 — A cleaner Dashboard, Layer 4 Review by rule with one card at a time, and capture and auto-clean rates

Date: 20 Nov 2026

The Dashboard now shows only what needs attention, and Layer 4 Review opens on the rules that hold items, with one card at a time.

## What's new

- The Dashboard is the role line, the five cards, Active failures and your 3 most recent items under Continue where you left off. Waiting for your approval, Waiting for you, Also and the four summary cards are gone; Layer 4 Review, Layer 1 Register and Platform Health are where that work is done.
- Active failures lists genuine failures and warnings only (a job whose last run failed, stuck work, a connection or check failing), worst first, each with the screen that fixes it.
- Layer 4 Review opens on Review by rule. Choose a queue; Waiting, grouped by rule, groups every waiting item of that queue by the rule that holds it, largest first, and the right shows one card at a time. Preview the batch lists the exact records of a rule. Only Contains a banned word can be admitted as one batch: each record with the marked words removed, one decision each with its reason, after you confirm. Every other rule, including every entry requirement rule, is decided card by card. By university, By kind, Grid and Decided are under Other views.
- Every held card opens with the amber Main hold reason (the ? opens the help; the machine reason is under Technical detail). Banned words are marked in yellow in the proposed text, the cleaned text sits above Remove "word" & Admit, and the action bar is Save Edit & Admit, Approve As-Is and Reject set apart on the right.
- Platform Health: Page Capture shows pages captured against the normal rate, and AI Extraction & Auto-Clean shows the auto-clean pass rate against normal, from the figures stored every 5 minutes.

## Fixed

- The Dashboard spend card showed "cap not set": it now shows today's spend against the US$14.00 daily cap, with its bar.

## Good to know

- Nothing is decided by grouping or previewing. Each decision is still recorded on its own, with who, when and why.
