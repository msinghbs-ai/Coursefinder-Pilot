# PIM Admin v2.15.339 — Idle lanes wake within minutes; a ledger of every worker call

Date: 24 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## What's new

- A lane the idle guard paused is tried again within "Idle lane: tried again within" (Settings › Pipeline settings › Waste rules, 15 minutes) and the moment work waits for it; before, a drained lane slept for six hours and more.
- Every worker call is written to a per-mode ledger (what it processed, how much succeeded and failed, where it landed); queue wait per lane is measured from the queues' own timestamps.
- Platform health › Work queues says in one line why each queue is idle.
- A fee from a course page whose proof the country's tuition policy refuses is quarantined at the write, whatever path brought it.

## Bug fixes

- The University of Sydney's course data files answered with the course page itself and were refused as "not JSON"; they are read as the page and the 219 affected files are re-read.
