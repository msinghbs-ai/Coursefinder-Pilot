# PIM Admin v2.15.394 — Fee checks show the proposed fee and are admitted or not

Date: 1 Nov 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Layer 4 Review, fee checks:** the card shows the proposed fee: amount, currency, whether it is a yearly or indicative yearly figure, the fee year (or that none is printed) and the audience.
  - The choices are **Admit this fee** and **Do not admit this fee**.
  - An address can no longer be entered on a fee check.
  - After a decision the result shows the fee now held by the course, with the decision and audit numbers.
- **Layer 4 Review, links:** an address that opens another kind or university now opens it on the same screen, without reloading the page.

## Bug fixes

- Entering an address on a fee check admitted the proposed fee instead of reading the page again. It is now refused.
