# PIM Admin v2.15.386 — Faster scholarship hold check; re-read pilot for exhausted course pages

Date: 31 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Platform health and the scholarship readers (StudySearch decision D16):** the check of whether a university is held by the scholarship Firecrawl rules keeps its query plans for the session instead of planning them on every call.
  - The rules and results are the same.
  - In a trial, checking every provider took 5.7 s instead of 20.1 s.
- **Course pages, re-read pilot (decision D13):** course pages that used all three read attempts get one more direct read.
  - At most 25 a day, only 30 days after the last attempt, and each page once.
  - No Firecrawl and no AI are used for them.
  - Held courses and restricted providers are skipped.
  - The pilot records each outcome. It switches itself off if a page is read through Firecrawl or produces Layer 4 review items.
- **Settings › Pipeline settings › Re-read and retry:** three settings for the pilot: on, pages a day (up to the approved 25), and days to wait (at least 30).

None.
