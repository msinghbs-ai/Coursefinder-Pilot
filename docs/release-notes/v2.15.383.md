# PIM Admin v2.15.383 — Health alerts that stay until fixed; true AI spend

Date: 31 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Platform health, scheduled jobs (StudySearch SS-014):** a job that keeps failing stays on the health list until it has run cleanly.
  - It becomes critical when its last three runs all failed, whatever its schedule.
  - Before, each failure closed itself within the hour, so hourly and daily jobs never escalated.
- **Platform health, AI spend:** spend over 24 hours now counts every OpenRouter route (enrichment, AI match, entry reads and Layer 3), measured from the credit balance.
  - The daily ceiling (US$14) is now a setting.
  - The health detail shows the remaining credit and the day's spend as separate figures.
- **Settings › Pipeline settings:** four new Platform upkeep settings hold these thresholds.
- **StudySearch name (SS-013):** test and workflow names now use StudySearch. Storage keys, identifiers and history are kept.
