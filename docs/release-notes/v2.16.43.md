## What's new

- Layer 4 Review › Inbox › Grid › Clusters: choose a kind and the waiting items are grouped by university, reason and the same quote (numbers, spacing and links ignored). Open a cluster to see its items, export the clusters, or settle ticked clusters with one choice, one reason and a typed confirmation. Each item is decided exactly as on its own card, at the rank it needs, and logged.
- Entry requirements, campus localities, provider departures and Human input needed are inspect only in Clusters. A choice that needs a value or a page is never offered for a whole cluster.
- A cluster that keeps coming back is the case for a Boost or reader rule: export it so the rule can be written into the worker and tested.
