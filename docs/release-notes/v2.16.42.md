# PIM Admin v2.16.42 — Coverage now, climate icons and how the data connects

Date: 18 Nov 2026

Platform Admin, 18 Nov 2026 12:19 and 14:23.

## What's new

- Coverage & completeness › Courses starts with Coverage now: readiness by country (AU, NZ, CA, UK, IE, US by Courses, Providers & campuses, AER & grade scales, Scholarships, Destination editions) and every recorded field with its share. A newly recorded field appears by itself, marked Not labelled until it has a label.
- Targets, the 7-day change, trend lines and Courses by fields admitted (0 of N to N of N) appear when the coverage read is live; its database change is with S2-Build.
- The portals that supply course links and the Stalled fields counters are retired from Coverage; Enrich fields keeps its stalled-field controls.
- Destination data › City metrics & costs: each climate figure shows an icon (sunny, partly cloudy, rain, heavy rain, storm, snow, wind). The figures are long-term averages, not forecasts.
- Guides › Platform guide › How the data connects: how destinations, providers, courses, study rules and career outcomes join, with the API v3 paths.
