# v2.15.486 — Course catalogue: choose your columns and see data freshness

Platform Admin mandate 5 Nov 2026 17:31; release authorised 22:52 (PR #887).

## What changes

- A Columns control: search, show or hide, reorder and resize columns; your layout is saved to your account and follows you to any device. Reset to default and Restore last saved.
- New columns: Adapter, City (from the course's campuses), Intake month, Intake year, Commencement date, Application deadline, Course record updated (as recorded), Last data admitted and Last published.
- Sort by Last data admitted or Adapter across the whole filtered list.

## Not changed

The consumer API, enrichment automation, admission rules and manual locks are unchanged. Search, filters and paging behave as before.
