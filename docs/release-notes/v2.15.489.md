# v2.15.489 — Provider descriptions: Run & Validate with evidence-checked claims

Platform Admin authorisation 6 Nov 2026 12:55 (PR #877).

## What changes

- Enrich fields has a **Provider descriptions Run & Validate** card: preview and save a small set of providers, and see the official source, what was read, proposed and held, and why.
- Every description still needs an individual **Layer 4** decision from a Pipeline Operator or higher. Nothing is approved, admitted or published automatically.
- A sentence about a campus, scholarship or guarantee is checked against its quoted source rather than held on the word alone.
- Proposals waiting for review space resume without another AI read.

## Not changed

- The existing reader, its approved AI model and its spending limits.
- Manual locks and the Website and Zoho consumer API.
