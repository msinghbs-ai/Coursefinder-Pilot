# PIM Admin v2.15.397 — Refusals and links name the role that acts

Date: 1 Nov 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Layer 4 Review:** a choice your role cannot make is shown disabled with the role that makes it.
- **Pages and tabs above your role** say which role opens them.
- **Automations:** a refusal names the role that changes the automation.
- **Platform guide:** links above your role say which role opens them.
- **Entry requirements:** the row drawer and the Read again column name the role that edits or re-reads.

## Bug fixes

- A Curator could fill in a Layer 4 decision that the server then refused.
- "Not authorised" pages said only "Ask a Platform Admin", and a tab above your role silently showed another tab.
- The error banner replaced the server's role message with a general sentence.
- "Read an entry requirement source again" opened the provider entry matrix instead of Sources, reads and holds.
