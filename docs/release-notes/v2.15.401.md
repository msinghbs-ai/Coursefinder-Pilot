# PIM Admin v2.15.401 — An incomplete entry-requirement read cannot be approved

Date: 2 Nov 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Entry requirement reads:** a read that finds more requirements than the course's limit is held whole, not cut short.
- **Layer 4 Review:** Approve is refused while a read is incomplete or mixes in another programme. The screen says why.
- **Settings:** the limits and checks are in Settings › Pipeline settings, Entry requirements.

## Bug fixes

- A course could be approved with only part of its entry requirements, or with another programme's requirements, from a shared page.
