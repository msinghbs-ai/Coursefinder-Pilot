# PIM Admin v2.15.391 — Layer 4 Review reaches every item, page by page

Date: 1 Nov 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Layer 4 Review:** a kind with more than 200 items now shows them 200 at a time, oldest first, with **Previous** and **Next** and the range (for example 201–400 of 993).
  - Every item can be reached.
  - A decision or a newly raised item does not move the list, so nothing is skipped or shown twice.
  - When you open a destination screen and come back, the list returns to the page you were on.

## Bug fixes

None.
