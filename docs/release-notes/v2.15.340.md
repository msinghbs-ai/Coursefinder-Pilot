# PIM Admin v2.15.340 — Hourly health rows

Date: 24 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## What's new

- Every hour at :11 the platform writes its health rows: pages read and values admitted against the headline target, field changes per country over the hour and the day, queue wait per lane, worker calls per mode, idle lanes with why, bound-but-empty pages, negative movers and budget burn. The hourly report reads them.
- The 15-minute stats cover the countries listed in Settings › Pipeline settings › Health report (Australia joins New Zealand and Canada).
