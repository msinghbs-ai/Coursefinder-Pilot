# PIM Admin v2.15.385 — Switching a job back on clears its idle pause

Date: 31 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Automations (StudySearch decision D14):** switching a job back on, for one job or a whole area, now also releases the automatic idle pause the flag unit had put on it.
  - The release is recorded as yours, with the reason given.
  - The flag unit can pause the job again if it goes idle.
- **AI page matching:** this job had been switched on by hand on 19 Oct while its idle pause stayed raised. That pause is now released, citing the 19 Oct restart.
- **Platform alerts:** checked every 10 minutes. One check an hour had been skipped.
