# PIM Admin v2.15.366 — Course evidence overlay, scholarships, entry levels, countries, phone fixes, NZ course reading

Date: 29 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Course detail evidence:**
  - The evidence button opens the course's evidence timeline in an overlay; on a phone it is full screen.
  - The timeline covers page discovery and reads, page changes, AI matches and fills, admissions and refusals, the fee reader, cross-checks, edits by a person, academic entry requirement reads and scholarship links. Each entry shows its date, outcome, source and evidence.
  - The blade no longer embeds "What happened to this course".
- **Course detail scholarships:**
  - The course's published scholarships show again (the course read had stopped returning them).
  - An empty list explains why and offers Check scholarships for this course.
- **Academic entry requirements:**
  - A published level label that names several levels counts for each, so a Diploma course finds a provider's "Diploma/Associate degrees" rows.
  - The explanation shown is the true one.
- **Countries:**
  - India, Nepal and Singapore come first, then A to Z, in Course detail and every Entry requirements country selector.
  - Course detail remembers the chosen country.
  - Changing country only re-reads the resolution; it never starts an AI read.
- **Phone:**
  - An Entry requirements row tap opens its drawer, and the drawer scrolls inside.
  - The low-credit notice wraps beside its icon.
- **Scholarship links:**
  - A page whose stated levels are not recognised no longer links every course by field alone.
  - Postgraduate research pages map to research levels.
- **New Zealand course reading:** course-level entry requirements can be read for New Zealand courses whose page carries an approved strong identity proof. A title-only match is never read.
- **Consumer API:** unchanged.
