# PIM Admin v2.16.66 — Clearer description review, automatic removal of banned words and a Platform Health summary

Date: 20 Nov 2026

Description cards say plainly why they are held, and a banned promotional word no longer parks a good description.

## What's new

- Each University and Course description card shows its main hold reason in plain English at the top, for example "Contains 1 banned word — innovative".
- When a banned word is the only problem, Remove "word" & Admit admits the text with the word removed and locks it by hand, in one click.
- One action bar per description card: Remove "word" & Admit, Save Edit & Admit, Approve As-Is and, set apart, Reject.
- University descriptions held only for a promotional word on the readers' list are admitted automatically with the word removed, after the same checks.
- Human Edits lists the words reviewers remove most often.
- Platform Health opens on a plain-English summary: overall health, AI spend in the last 24 hours against the daily cap, active blockers and Layer 4 items waiting, then Needs attention (failed jobs, stuck work, connections down). Everything technical is under Technical details.

## Good to know

- Only promotional words are removed automatically (for example "innovative", "leading", "world-class"). Words such as "campus" or "fee" stay with a person.
