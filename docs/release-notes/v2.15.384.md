# PIM Admin v2.15.384 — Faster health checks, search refresh watch, alerts ready, weekly scorecard

Date: 31 Oct 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## Changes

- **Platform health, scholarship check (StudySearch SS-014A):** it works out each provider's hold once instead of up to three times. The results are the same.
  - Live, the check took 7.0 to 8.5 s in its first three runs, against 11.6 to 13.7 s before.
  - The whole health run is still about 22 s. The 24-hour measurement on 1 Nov will confirm the change.
- **Platform health, paused jobs:** a work queue whose job the flag unit paused after idle runs is shown for information while the flag unit switches it on again.
  - It becomes critical only after two hours.
  - It had been raised as critical 116 times in a week, each time normal operation.
- **Platform health, search refresh (SS-014B):** a new check for search refresh requests.
  - A request waiting past 40 minutes is a warning that names the cause (the refresh gap or another writer).
  - A request waiting past 90 minutes, or one whose job is missing, paused or failing, is critical and comes with the next step.
- **Platform health › Alerts (SS-014C):** open critical issues become alerts.
  - Each check has a quiet period; an issue that becomes critical is escalated; an open issue is reminded; a recovery notice follows.
  - Failed deliveries are retried and then shown as failed.
  - Delivery outside the platform is off until the Platform Admin approves a destination; alerts are recorded on this page meanwhile.
  - A Platform Admin can acknowledge an issue with a reason, and send a test alert.
- **Platform health › Weekly operational scorecard (SS-014D):** targets T1 to T8 measured every morning from existing data.
  - Each target says whether it was observed for the whole week, so far, or only at report time.
- **Settings › Pipeline settings:** new Alerts and Operating targets sections, and two search refresh thresholds under Platform upkeep.

## Bug fixes

None.
