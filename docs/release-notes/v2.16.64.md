# PIM Admin v2.16.64 — Bulk queue to intake, bulk flag primary campus and Human Locked pills

Date: 20 Nov 2026

The Providers list gets two more actions on its bar for ticked providers, and the drawers show which fields a person has locked.

## What's new

- Queue to Intake Run & Validate hands the ticked providers to Layer 2/3 Operations. Each is chosen in turn for the usual preview, fix and run. Nothing is run by the hand-off.
- Flag primary campus (Platform Admin) previews, then marks the campus with the most active courses as the primary campus for the ticked providers, after you give a reason. Providers that already have one, have it locked, tie or are outside Australia, New Zealand and Canada are skipped and listed.
- A Human Locked pill marks every tracked field a person has locked in the Provider and Course drawers.

## Good to know

- Register and AI verified are shown for the description only. The other fields do not record their source yet.
