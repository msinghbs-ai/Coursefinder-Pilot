# StudySearch – Viewer guide

**Version:** 1.0 (reviewed for platform release v2.17.0)  
**Role:** Viewer (rank 1)  
**Change control:** CF-247  
**Source:** generated from the Pilot `src/guide/roleGuides.js` and the menu; never edited by hand.

## 1. What this role is for

Read and search the catalogue: courses, providers, campuses, scholarships, entry requirements, rankings and how complete each record is.

## 2. What you can do

- Search and filter every course, provider, campus and scholarship list, and open any record with its sources.
- Read entry requirements by country of prior education, and rankings and statistics side by side.
- Export a list you are looking at as CSV.
- Pick up a search or page you left from Dashboard › Continue where you left off.

## 3. What you cannot do

- Change any value, status or setting. Nothing you do changes data.
- Open Layer 1 to Layer 4, Destination data, Reference data, Platform health, Scheduled jobs, Settings or Users & roles.

## 4. Pages you can open

- **Courses**
- **Providers**: Providers, Campuses
- **Scholarships**: Scholarships
- **Entry requirements**: Provider entry matrix, Course entry requirements, Sources, reads and holds, Resolution inspection
- **Rankings & statistics**: Overview, Compare, Datasets
- **Coverage & completeness**: Courses, Attributes
- **Provider contacts**
- **Guides**: Platform guide, Role guides

## 5. Where to start

- Dashboard: type what you are looking for in Search the catalogue, or open Courses, Providers or Scholarships.
- Courses: filter by study level, field, city or provider; open a course to see its fees, intakes, English and entry requirements and where each came from.

## 6. Common tasks

### Find a course

1. Open Courses.
2. Type part of the title, or filter by study level, field of study, city or provider.
3. Open the course: each value shows the page it was read from.

### Compare providers

1. Open Rankings & statistics › Compare.
2. Choose the providers; QS, THE, QILT and PRISMS figures sit side by side with their edition.

## 7. When to ask someone

Ask a Platform Admin when you need to correct a value or need more access: your role is changed on Users & roles.
