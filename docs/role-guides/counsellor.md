# StudySearch – Counsellor guide

**Version:** 1.0 (reviewed for platform release v2.16.35)  
**Role:** Counsellor (rank 2)  
**Change control:** CF-247  
**Source:** generated from the Pilot `src/guide/roleGuides.js` and the menu; never edited by hand.

## 1. What this role is for

Find the right course and scholarship for a student, and check what they need to get in: English, academic entry and study load.

## 2. What you can do

- Everything a Viewer can.
- Use course search for a student: study level, field, city, fees, intakes, study load (full-time, part-time or both) and English requirement.
- Read entry requirements for the student's country of prior education, and the scholarships open to international students.
- Pick up the searches you were working on from Dashboard › Continue where you left off.

## 3. What you cannot do

- Change any value, status or setting.
- Decide review items, approve, publish or run jobs. Those are Curator, operator and Platform Admin work.

## 4. Pages you can open

- **Courses**
- **Providers**: Providers, Campuses
- **Scholarships**: Scholarships
- **Entry requirements**: Provider entry matrix, Course entry requirements, Sources, reads and holds, Resolution inspection
- **Rankings & statistics**: Overview, Compare, Datasets
- **Coverage & completeness**: Courses, Attributes
- **Provider contacts**
- **Guides**: Platform guide, Role guides

## 5. Where to start

- Dashboard: Search the catalogue, then narrow the list with the filters on Courses.
- A course's study load and empty reason say what the provider states and why a value is missing; a missing value is never guessed.

## 6. Common tasks

### Shortlist courses for a student

1. Open Courses and filter by study level, field of study and city.
2. Add Study load from Columns to see full-time or part-time.
3. Open each course to check fees, intakes, English and entry requirements for the student's country.

### Find scholarships

1. Open Scholarships.
2. Filter to international students and the study level; each scholarship links to the provider's own page.

## 7. When to ask someone

Spotted a wrong value? Tell a Curator or Platform Admin with the course and what the provider's page says.
