# PIM Admin v2.15.88 — Layer 4 team and forecast

Date: 24 Sep 2026
State: release candidate (v2.15.79 remains the accepted recovery release)

## What's new

- Team and forecast view: waiting items, arrivals and decisions per day, waiting times, and when the queue clears (or a warning that it is growing).
- Managers see who decided what, with median time per decision; everyone else sees their own figures.
- Task filters show how many items are waiting.
- Tuition items with no proposed fee show Edit and approve as the main action.

## Bug / UI fixes

- Tuition items with no proposed fee showed only Reject and More; Edit and approve is back.
- Approving a tuition fee from the app no longer fails with "UPDATE requires a WHERE clause".
- Course details no longer time out.
